Core Concepts

Armies, Squads & Models

Armies in Bloodbloom are broken down into squads. A squad represents a single coherent fighting force — selected for activation together, with every model in the squad taking the same ActionAction
Actions are selected when a squad is activated and dictate what it does for the phase. Types: Instant, Prepare, Reaction..

Squads from your army are considered friendly; squads from another player's army are opposing.

Models are the individual troops, tanks, and commanders that make up your army. Every model in your army belongs to a squad, though this may be a squad of one. Models have attributes (unmodified statsheet values) and values (attributes including any active modifications).

Example
A model may have a Movement attribute of 6″, but including a Wound Marker penalty it has a Movement value of 4″.

Heroes

Heroes are the champions, leaders, and specialist operatives of your army — typically a squad of one powerful model with unique abilities.

During the Movement Phase, a Hero may join a friendly squad by moving to within Close-Combat RangeClose-Combat Range
Within 1″ horizontally, measured base-to-base, with no more than 5″ height difference. of it. A Hero may only be within Close-Combat Range of a single friendly squad at any time — movement that would bring it within range of two or more is invalid.

While leading a squad, a Hero is considered part of the squad: it uses the squad's attributes and shares the squad's abilities and tagsTags
Classifications like infantry. On their own tags do nothing, but abilities and rules often reference them. (but not traits). It may receive Wound Markers. If the Hero is destroyed, or if every other model in the squad is destroyed, it counts as a destroyed squad.

Note
If a Hero is leading a squad, add a +2 bonus to any Command Tests the squad makes.

Cohesion

A squad is Cohesive if every model is horizontally within 1″ of at least one other model in the squad, and a continuous chain can be traced connecting any two models. After resolving wounds, the controlling player must remove models until cohesion is restored if it was broken.

Locked-in-Combat, Close-Combat Range & Line of Sight

A squad is Locked-in-Combat with another squad if any of its models are within Close-Combat Range of an opposing model in that squad.

Close-Combat Range: Within 1″ horizontally (base-to-base) with a height difference of no more than 5″.

Line of Sight is determined from the model's head (or geometric centre if no obvious head). At least 25% of the target model or its base must be visible. Models can see through their own squad but not through other squads.

Cover

A targeted squad has Cover against a given model if ≥50% of the models in the targeted squad each have at least 25% of their model obscured by terrain.

Ranged Attacks made against a squad with Cover suffer a −1 penalty to hit.

Objectives

Victory Points are gained by completing Objectives. A player reaching 10 Victory Points wins. There are two types:

Battlefield Objectives

Shared goals both armies vie for. May be Progressive (recurring VP each time conditions are met) or Tactical (scored once per army). Only one Battlefield Objective is revealed at match start; an additional one is revealed at the end of each Strategic Phase.

Agendas

Hidden objectives known only to your army. Each Agenda can only be scored once. They are revealed to your opponent only upon scoring. Routing units may not score either type of objective.

Orders

Orders are special actions players can use at any time. There is no limit on how often an Order can be played, but you have a finite pool of Order Points (OP) each Round.

Order Points

At the start of each round, gain 1 OP. Maximum pool is 5 OP; excess is lost. Additional OP may be gained from strategic squads on the battlefield.

Standard Orders

The following Orders are available to all players regardless of faction.

Reroll 1 OP
Phase: Any

After any single test of dice has been rolled by you, reroll all dice and take the new result. If a test called for 2d6, you reroll both dice.

Sprint 1 OP
Phase: Movement Phase

When a squad is selected for an action allowing them to move, roll a d6 and add the result to their movement value. May only be used once per squad per phase.

Sequence Break 2 OP
Phase: Movement or Engagement Phase

After a squad has finished their action but before you end your turn, you may activate another squad.

Bravery 2 OP
Phase: Any

Before a squad takes a Command TestCommand Test
Roll 3d6 vs. the squad's Leadership value. Equal to or above = pass., they automatically pass instead.

Update Directives 3 OP
Phase: Strategic Phase (Agenda Step)

Discard an unscored Agenda and draw a new one.

Ready 4 OP
Phase: Engagement Phase

Before activating a squad on your turn, remove the Activation Token from an already-activated squad to ready them.

Actions & Activations

When a squad is selected during the Activation Step of a phase, it is activated until the end of the phase (or until readied by a rule). It must be assigned a relevant Action it meets the requirements for. An Activation Token is placed next to the squad after its Action concludes. An activated squad cannot be selected again until its Activation Token is removed.

Instant Actions

Resolved immediately upon activation with generally few requirements.

Prepare Actions

Do not resolve immediately — they set a squad up for a later payoff. A reminder marker is placed (functionally as an Activation Token) and replaced with an Activation Token when the action resolves.

Interruptible: If a squad performing an Interruptible Prepare Action is targeted by a Ranged Attack, after the attack concludes the squad must make a Command Test with a −1 penalty per Hit scored. Failure, or becoming Locked-in-CombatLocked-in-Combat
A squad is locked-in-combat when any of its models are within Close-Combat Range of an opposing model., replaces the reminder marker with an Activation Token.

Reactions

Declared on another player's turn in response to a trigger. The squad is considered activated upon declaring the Reaction. Each Reaction specifies a trigger (what causes it) and a timing (when it resolves).

Movement

When instructed to move, a model may move in a series of unobstructed straight lines up to its Movement value. It may not move through terrain or models not in its squad unless specified.

Obstacles

Terrain ≤ 2″ tall is an Obstacle — models may move through it at the cost of −2″ movement. Terrain taller than 2″ cannot be moved through, but may be climbed by models with the infantry tag, provided they have sufficient movement to traverse the vertical and horizontal distance and can fit their base on the surface.

Pivots

Models on non-circular bases may rotate during movement at a cost of −1″ per rotation, always about the geometric centre of the base.

Flying

Models with the Flying trait move through the air in 3-dimensional space. They must finish on a surface that can fit their entire base. Measure direct 3D movement; when flying up, measure to the surface edge then add horizontal movement.

Command Test

Roll 3d6 and compare the result to the squad's Leadership value, applying any bonuses or penalties. If the result ≥ Leadership value, the squad passes; otherwise it fails.

Order of Play

Rounds proceed through sequential phases, each divided into an Activation Step and a Resolution Step. Starting with the player holding the Initiative Token, players alternate activating squads one at a time.

If one player runs out of unactivated squads, the other player continues activating sequentially. Once both players have exhausted their squads, the phase enters its Resolution Step: Hit Markers are resolved, Activation Tokens removed, and the next phase begins.

Movement Phase

Players position their squads across the battlefield. Available actions during the Activation Step:

Hold Fast Instant

The squad makes no movement. Each model remains in place.

Move Instant
Requirements: Squad may not be Locked-in-Combat.

Each model may move up to its movement value. Models may not pass through or end within Close-Combat RangeClose-Combat Range
Within 1″ horizontally, base-to-base, with ≤5″ height difference. of an opposing squad.

Regroup Instant
Requirements: Squad must be Locked-in-Combat.

The squad takes a Command TestCommand Test
Roll 3d6 vs. Leadership threshold. Pass: equal to or above. Fail: below.. On failure, they cannot move. On success, each model moves up to its movement value, ending outside Close-Combat Range of all opposing squads.

Pounce Reaction
Trigger: An opposing player Regroups or Routes a squad Locked-in-Combat with one of your unactivated squads.
Timing: After the opposing squad's Command Test, before any movement.

Your Locked-in-Combat squad makes a Melee Opportunity Attack against the triggering squad. Multiple squads may Pounce the same trigger; all must be declared before any attack is resolved.

Deployment Phase

Reinforcements arrive; troops embark and disembark from transports. Squads are not activated in this phase. Players alternate through the Embarking Step then the Reinforcing Step.

Transports

Models with the Transport trait may carry other models. Embarked models cannot be targeted, but may make Ranged Attacks under certain conditions. A transport has a specific tag requirement and a capacity (sum of embarked model sizes).

Embark Instant
Requirements: Every model wholly within 3″ of target transport; squad not Locked-in-Combat.

Remove all models (and their Markers) from the board — they are now inside the transport.

Disembark Instant
Requirements: Squad currently within a transport.

Place all models wholly within 3″ of the transport. Any opposing model entering Close-Combat Range may make a Melee Opportunity Attack against the placed squad.

Transport Destroyed
If a transport is destroyed while carrying troops, roll 1d3 for each embarked model before removing it. On a 2+, generate a Wound Marker for the squad.

Engagement Phase

Squads fight for tactical superiority through ranged volleys and brazen charges.

Fire! Instant
Requirements: Squad not Locked-in-Combat; must have at least one eligible ranged weapon.

Each model may perform a Ranged Attack.

Charge! Instant
Requirements: Squad must be Locked-in-Combat; must have at least one melee weapon.

Each model may perform a Melee Attack.

Dash Instant
Requirements: Squad not Locked-in-Combat. Limit: Only one Melee Attack per squad from this action.

Each model may move up to its movement value. If this brings the squad into Close-Combat Range with an opposing squad, they may make a Melee Attack with a +1 to hit bonus.

Ready Blades Prepare (Interruptible)
Requirements: Squad must be Locked-in-Combat; must have at least one melee weapon.

Place a Readied Marker next to the squad. While active, if the squad becomes Locked-in-Combat with an opposing squad, replace the marker with an Activation Token and immediately take Charge! with a +1 to hit bonus.

Overwatch Prepare (Interruptible)
Requirements: Squad not Locked-in-Combat; must have at least one ranged weapon.

Place an Overwatch Marker. While active, the squad may use the Snapshot reaction but makes a normal Ranged Attack instead of an Opportunity Attack. Resolving the Snapshot replaces the marker with an Activation Token.

Retreat Prepare (Interruptible)
Requirements: Squad wholly within your deployment area.

Place a Retreat Marker. At the end of the Resolution Step, if the squad is still wholly within your deployment area, you may remove all remaining models — add them as a new squad to your reinforcements with all Wound and status Markers removed.

Snapshot Reaction
Trigger: An opposing squad moves within line of sight of one of your unactivated squads.
Requirements: Activated squad not Locked-in-Combat.
Timing: After the triggering squad finishes movement.

Each model may perform a Ranged Opportunity Attack against the triggering squad. If no model can make the attack, the action is refunded. The Opportunity Attack is assumed to occur at the most beneficial moment during the triggering movement — if the squad had any moment out of cover and in LoS, it may be targeted accordingly.

Defend Reaction
Trigger: An opposing player Dashes into Close-Combat Range with one of your unactivated squads.
Timing: After the opposing squad's movement, before any Melee Attack.

For the duration of the phase, if the triggering squad makes a Melee Attack against this squad, they do so with a −2 to hit penalty.

Hunker Down Reaction
Trigger: An opposing player targets one of your unactivated squads with a Ranged Attack.
Requirements: Activated squad not Locked-in-Combat.
Timing: After targets are declared, before the attack is performed.

For the duration of the phase, if this squad has Cover against an attacking model making a Ranged Attack, that model gains an additional −1 to hit ranged penalty on top of Cover's normal penalty.

Leadership Phase

Commanders attempt to rally troops after the round's carnage.

Morale Step

For each squad that gained any Wound Markers this round: compare the number of unwounded models to the number of Wound Markers generated this round. If Wound Markers ≥ unwounded models, the squad must make a Command Test with a penalty equal to that difference.

On failure, the squad begins Routing. Routing squads may only take the Rout action and cannot score objectives.

Rout Instant
Requirements: Squad must be Routing.

Each model must move its full movement value toward the controlling player's deployment edge via the shortest path. No model may end within Close-Combat Range of an opposing squad — if impossible, remove the model.

After movement: if the squad is wholly within the controlling player's deployment area it is destroyed. Otherwise it makes a Command Test — on a pass it is no longer Routing.

Disband Step

Heroes leading squads may depart. A Hero may move up to 1″, ending outside Close-Combat Range of any friendly squad and not entering Close-Combat Range of any opposing squad it wasn't already engaged with.

Strategic Phase

During the Agenda Step, players may declare scored Agendas. In the Resolution Step, the next unrevealed Battlefield Objective is revealed and the Initiative Token passes to the other player.

Ending the Match
The match ends when a player reaches 10 Victory Points. If both players reach 10 simultaneously, continue until a player would gain VP alone. If that too is simultaneous, the match is a draw.

Combat

Ranged Combat

The model selects a ranged weapon and a target squad that is visible and within range, then performs the Combat Sequence.

Melee Combat

If not already in Close-Combat Range, the model may first move up to 3″ toward an opposing model. It then selects a melee weapon and a target in Close-Combat Range that is visible.

Models without a melee weapon may use an Improvised Attack: Range Melee, Attacks 1, Penetration Power 0, Stopping Power 1.

When selecting a melee weapon the model may opt for Sweeping Attacks: double the number of attacks, but lower Penetration and Stopping Power by one step each.

Opportunity Attacks

All Opportunity Attacks carry an innate −2 to hit penalty due to their hasty nature. Ranged Opportunity Attacks are assumed to occur at the most advantageous moment during the triggering movement.

Combat Sequence — Activation Step

Rolling to Hit

Roll 1d6. Add bonuses and penalties. If the result ≥ the model's To Hit value (Aim for Ranged, Prowess for Melee), a hit is scored.

The threshold may be modified past 7+ (impossible to hit) but never below 2+ (a roll of 1 always misses).

Modifier Value
Melee Attack after Dashing into combat +1 to hit
Melee Attack against opponents Defending −2 to hit
Ranged Attack against squad in Cover −1 to hit
Ranged Attack against squad Hunkering Down in Cover −2 to hit
Any Opportunity Attack −2 to hit

Firing into Melee

When targeting a squad Locked-in-Combat with a friendly squad, the attacking squad must first pass a Command TestCommand Test
Roll 3d6 vs. Leadership threshold. Equal to or above = pass.. On failure, another eligible target must be chosen (not Locked-in-Combat with friendlies). If none exist, the squad ends its activation without attacking.

On success, after determining hits, roll 1d6 per hit and compare friendly vs. enemy numbers to determine friendly fire:

Relative Numbers Friendly Hit Range
Target has ≥ 3× as many models as friendly locked 1
Target has more models, but fewer than 3× 1–2
Target and friendly have equal models 1–3
Friendly has more models, but fewer than 3× 1–4
Friendly has ≥ 3× as many models as target 1–5

Hits in the Friendly Hit Range are allocated to the friendly squad. The attacking squad gains a −1 Command Test penalty for each Hit Marker generated by friendly fire this round.

Rolling against Armour

Compare the weapon's Penetration Power (PP) to the target squad's Armour Rating (AR):

  • PP ≥ AR → attack automatically succeeds.
  • PP < AR → roll 1d6 per hit, compare to the threshold below.
PP vs AR Success Threshold
PP is 1 step less than AR 4+
PP is 2 steps less than AR 5+
PP is 3 steps less than AR 6+
PP is 4+ steps less than AR Cannot succeed

On success, adjust Stopping Power:

  • PP ≤ AR: −1 Stopping Power.
  • PP exceeds AR by 2+ steps: +1 Stopping Power per 2 steps.

Each successful attack generates a Hit Marker (represented by a die set to the Stopping Power value).

Combat Sequence — Resolution Step

Hit Markers are allocated one at a time to models by the controlling player. Compare Stopping Power (SP) to the model's Toughness (T) and roll 2d3:

SP vs T Wound Threshold
SP is 3+ steps below Toughness Cannot wound
SP is 2 steps below Toughness 6+
SP is 1 step below Toughness 5+
SP equals Toughness 4+
SP exceeds Toughness by 1 step 3+
SP exceeds Toughness by 2+ steps Auto-wound

A successful wound places a Wound Marker on the model. When Wound Markers ≥ Endurance the model is destroyed. Each Wound Marker applies:

  • −1 to Aim and Prowess values (stacking).
  • −2″ to Movement value (stacking).

After all Wound Markers are applied and destroyed models removed, check squad Cohesion — remove models as needed to restore it.

Reference — Squad Traits

Sentinel X

A squad with this trait may make an additional X Snapshot reactions when taking the Overwatch action before replacing the Overwatch Marker with an Activation Token. (Place X+1 Overwatch Markers; remove one after each Snapshot.)

Guard X″

After an opposing squad finishes a movement within X″ of this squad (while it has the Readied Marker from Ready Blades and is not Locked-in-Combat), you may move each model up to X″ — at least one model must finish within Close-Combat Range of the opposing squad.

Reference — Model Traits

War Machine

Does not suffer standard Wound Marker penalties. Instead, whenever a Wound Marker is assigned, roll 1d6 and add (Stopping Power − Toughness) of the attack, then consult the War Machine damage table.

Transport X Y…

May carry up to X total size worth of models with at least one tag listed in Y.

Hover

May pass over Obstacles without the −2″ movement penalty.

Jump X″

May ignore up to X″ of vertical distance when climbing terrain.

Reference — Weapon Traits

Pinpoint

When allocating Hit Markers from this weapon, the attacking player chooses which model resolves the wound (instead of the controlling player).

Mounted

May also be selected to make a Ranged Attack when the bearer selects a ranged weapon. May target the same or a different squad.

Coaxial

May also be selected to make a Ranged Attack when the bearer selects a ranged weapon. Must target the same squad.

Explosive X

On a hit, roll 1d6 per model in the opposing squad — on X+, generate an extra hit. When Firing into Melee, also roll for each model in friendly squads locked in with the target.

Flamer dX

On a hit, roll dX and generate that many additional hits against the opposing squad.

Stun X

After a squad receives any Hit Markers from this weapon, roll 1d6. On X+, place an Activation Token on the opposing squad. If one already exists, they only remove one at end of phase (starting next Movement or Engagement Phase activated). Can only trigger once per round per squad.

Mortar

May target squads the bearer cannot see, provided at least one other friendly model has line of sight. Such attacks are made with a −2 to hit penalty.

Cull

Increase this weapon's Stopping Power by 1 against targets with an Armour Rating of 1 or less.

Ignores Armour

Hits from this weapon automatically become Hit Markers — no armour roll required.

Ignores Cover

Attacks from this weapon do not suffer to-hit penalties against targets in Cover or Hunkering Down.
